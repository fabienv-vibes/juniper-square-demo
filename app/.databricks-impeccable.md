## Design Context

### Surface type
Databricks App (Streamlit) running on Databricks Apps workspace runtime.

### Audience
External customer (Juniper Square — real estate investment management platform; product + engineering leadership). Presented live by a Databricks SA during platform-evaluation meetings.

### Theme
Light. Executive presentation context, projected or shown on a laptop screen. Dark mode not a priority for initial version.

### Use case
Present Databricks technical benchmark results (concurrency/latency/cost) framed inside Juniper Square's own 7-pillar technical-maturity scorecard. The scorecard acts as the narrative spine: each pillar shows where their current Redshift+ProServe stack lands vs. where Databricks moves them on day one, backed by live evidence where applicable (benchmark numbers, system tables, UC lineage).

### Brand (locked)
- Palette: Lava 600 #FF3621 (10% accent max), Navy 900 #0B2026 text, Light #F9F7F4 surface
- Typography: DM Sans 400/500/700 + DM Mono 400/500
- Components: Du Bois first; custom only when Du Bois doesn't ship it
- Voice: Databricks editorial-plain (plain statements, no superlatives, no marketing words)

### Additional constraints
- Presenter resolution: 1440×900 (laptop) and up to 1920×1080 (projected). Must have zero horizontal scrollbars at 1440×900.
- Performance: initial page load under 3s on warm warehouse. Each drill-in page under 1s after initial.
- Plotly for charts (polar radar, line charts, stacked bars). Plotly theming should match the locked palette and DM Sans typography; no default Plotly blues/greens except where semantically meaningful (stage colors: POC=red, MVP=orange, Scale=blue, Mature=green — these are intentional category encoding, not palette choices).
- The word "Juniper Square" must never appear in the logo slot; use Databricks wordmark only.
- Stage-chip colors for POC/MVP/Scale/Mature are semantic (map to the Lovable scorecard convention) and exempt from the 10% Lava rule.
